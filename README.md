Most of my work starts with a public system that is hard to read: a mutual-fund statement, a food-safety inspection record, a pile of MPLADS spending. ## 01 / Selected work

### Folio X-Ray

**A private X-ray for an Indian mutual-fund portfolio.**

Most trackers want a signup and a statement upload before they show you anything. Folio X-Ray does the arithmetic in the browser instead: XIRR with real cash-flow dates, fund overlap, the Regular-vs-Direct cost gap, and a range of goal outcomes. Add a fund, enter units or a SIP, read the report. Your holdings never leave the tab.

`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · custom XIRR / SIP engine · `Vitest`

### SafeBite

**Food-safety intelligence for FSSAI and Maharashtra FDA.**

A Smart India Hackathon 2026 build. SafeBite scores businesses into risk tiers, maps district-level risk, routes citizen complaints with an SLA clock and auto-escalation, and gives inspectors a prioritised queue with AI-drafted summaries. Three roles, one source of truth.

`Next.js` · `React` · `Prisma` · `SQLite` · `Leaflet` · `Recharts`

[Live demo ↗](https://foodshield-ai.vercel.app) · [Source ↗](https://github.com/digantshetty0006-ship-it/SafeBite-FoodSafety)

### Drishti

**A risk and investigation console for public works.**

Drishti reads MPLADS project data and runs twelve anomaly detectors over the fields the public record actually contains — cost, timeline, geography, relationships and data quality. Every score comes with its evidence. Officials get an investigation queue, district health scores, an interactive map and PDF/XLSX case packs. It is decision support, not a finding of fraud: the official makes the call.

`React` · `FastAPI` · `PostgreSQL` + `pgvector` · `scikit-learn` · `XGBoost`

[Live demo ↗](https://drishti-bitecoder.vercel.app) · [Source ↗](https://github.com/digantshetty0006-ship-it/Drishti)

## 02 / TWELVE

**Twelve months. One hand. Three wrong taps and the year is gone.**

A one-button timing game played on a year dial. The hand sweeps through 365 days; you lock each month as it passes. Hit the middle of a month to keep it clear. Keep all twelve and the year is yours.

Hand-written SVG, vanilla JavaScript and the Web Audio API. No framework, no backend, no tracking. Your best year stays in `localStorage`.

## 05 / How I work

1. **Make the number inspectable.** If I can't show the formula, the date and the assumption behind a figure, it doesn't go on the screen.
2. **Design the empty state first.** The first visit is usually nothing: no data, no account, no results. That screen has to be useful before the product is.
3. **Keep the blast radius small.** Local-first where it makes sense. No signup until it earns one. No dependency without a reason.
